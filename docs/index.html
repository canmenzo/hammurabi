What it does

Captions for sound that has none

System audio

Films, streams, lectures and calls. If your computer plays it, Hammurabi captions it.

Microphone

Talking to someone in person? Captions appear as they speak, even across a language gap.

Optional translation

Show the original line, a translated line, or both together while you learn.

Always on top overlay

Captions float above every window, including fullscreen. Move them where you like.

Tray and hotkey

It lives in the tray and stays out of the way. One global hotkey toggles captions.

Free and local

Whisper runs on your computer. No subscription, no account, no API key.

Sound should not stop at the language you speak. Hammurabi turns every voice on your computer into text you can read, and never sends a second of it anywhere.

How it works

Three steps, then it stays out of sight

  1. Pick a sourceChoose system audio, your microphone, or both from the tray menu.
  2. Pick a languageLeave it on automatic, or name the spoken language for better accuracy. Add a target language to translate.
  3. Press the hotkeyCaptions appear in the overlay. Press the hotkey again to hide them. The app keeps running in the tray.

Privacy

Your audio stays on your computer

Speech recognition and translation both run locally, so nothing you hear or say is uploaded for processing. There is no account to create and no server of ours in the loop. The code is public on GitHub, so you can check it yourself.

Status

Being built in the open

In development. Hammurabi is a Rust app and there is no download yet. Follow or star the repository to hear when the first build lands. Windows, macOS and Linux are the targets.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can I download it today?

Not yet. It is in development. Follow the GitHub repository to be first to know.

Will it be free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no trial and no API key to buy.

Does it send my audio anywhere?

No. Whisper runs on your computer, and so does translation. Nothing is uploaded.

Which systems will it support?

Windows, macOS and Linux, as a tray app.

What is the difference from Babil?

Babil captions videos inside your browser. Hammurabi captions the whole computer and your microphone, so it also works for native apps, calls and conversations.

Why the name?

Hammurabi ruled Babylon, and Babil is the Turkish name for Babylon. The desktop app is the sibling of the browser extension.
